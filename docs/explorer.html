Fictional examples

Explore a car park

Two datasets from this draft, drawn from the coordinates stored in the files. Simple is one surface lot. Complex is Merriwa Park, the fictional mall with three car parks. This is a v0.3 draft. No organisation has endorsed it.

Example data only. Wattle Reserve Office and Merriwa Park Shopping Centre, their roads, prices and coordinates are invented. A missing shape means that object has no coordinates in the file. The field text in the panel is copied from the schema descriptions.

Example

Tab to a shape or a tree row. Enter or Space selects it. The panel shows that object’s JSON and each field’s schema description. The same objects are listed under the map.
